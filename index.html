Workflow design

What the n8n workflow does

1. Capture

The website form sends lead data to an n8n Webhook URL.

2. Qualify

n8n scores the lead based on budget, timeline, company size, source, and problem.

3. Act

The business version appends to Google Sheets and prepares/sends sales follow-up actions.

Website form → n8n Webhook → Lead scoring logic → Google Sheet row → Sales alert → Follow-up email draft
